# MIRACULUM EDERE
> *"To perform a mirace..."*

Miraculum Edere is a content mod for Opus Magnum, attempting to unify the mods of others into a singular package, whilst also adding its own content to fill in the gaps. 

This mod is **not** recommended for your first venture into modding, due to how many dependancies it has and how many it will likely have in the future. It is recommended to be familiar with the dependant mods first before downloading this mod. 

## Metallurgy
The second-order metals from [Uncommon Alchemical Primes](https://github.com/Wheattttt/UncommonAlchemicalPrimes) are expanded upon and re-inforced as not being another set of half-metals. A new set of quicksilver variants, with metallicities from 2 to -2, have also been added, allowing promotion into the anti-metals. You can also shear vaca.

## Neuvolics
Volics and Neumetals from [Neuvolics](https://github.com/ErikHaag/Neuvolics) are no longer seperate sets! 2 glyphs are added to allow conversion between the two, albeit in esoteric ways.

## Tria Primae
Combine Salt, Quicksilver and Sulfur from [PrimaMateria](https://github.com/Wheattttt/PrimaMateria) into other atoms, like Zephiron, Quickgold or Calcite! Just... dont combine Sulfur with something that isnt itself for now. You can also combine Volatiles to create [Noble Elements.](https://github.com/RedstoneParadox/OMNobleElements). Yeah, that's right. I remembered that mod. 

## Animismus
Judge the morality and mortality from [False Aether](https://github.com/ErikHaag/FalseAether), rotating it clockwise, and... no, wait, thats it. Uh, huh.

## Cardinality
Duplicate onto Calcite from [True Salt](https://github.com/cloverpepsi/TrueSalt) to create a new family of cardinal-likes, which can be further transmuted into the [Crystallines!](https://github.com/trines-alucia/ComplicatedElements) or a new Quintessence-like. Also, 2 new atoms are added, the midpoints between Hot and Cold and Wet and Dry. No, these arent new ordinals.
